Beach Day

Pour the sand until every bottle is one color.

Levels

Level 1

How to play

  • Each bottle holds four layers of sand. Sort them so every bottle is one color (or empty).
  • Tap a bottle to pick it up, then tap another bottle to pour its top layer in.
  • Sand can go into an empty bottle, or onto sand of the same color, as long as there's room.
  • Matching layers on top pour together, as many as will fit.
  • No timer and no way to lose. Undo as much as you like, or Restart the level.
  • Stuck? Hint tells you a good next pour and why. Spare adds one extra empty bottle for the level.
  • On a computer: number keys 1 to 9 pick bottles, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes, H gives a hint.
