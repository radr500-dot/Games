How to play

  • Fill every square with a sun or a moon .
  • Each row and column gets the same number of suns and moons (3 and 3 on a 6 × 6 board).
  • No three in a row: never three suns or three moons side by side, across or down.
  • An = sign between two squares means they match. A ≠ sign means they're opposites.
  • Tap a square once for a sun, twice for a moon, and again to clear it. Shaded squares come with the puzzle and can't be changed.
  • Stuck? Hint explains the next step and why it works.
  • On a computer: right-click goes backwards (moon first). Arrow keys move, Space cycles a square, S places a sun, M places a moon, Backspace clears, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes, H gives a hint.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by logic alone, no guessing.
