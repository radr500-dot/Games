How to play

  • Place exactly one bloom in every row, every column and every colored plot.
  • They need space: two blooms can never touch, not even corner to corner.
  • Stuck? Hint explains the next step and why it works.
  • Tap a square once to mark it with an ✕ (your note for "not here"). Tap again to place a bloom, and again to clear it.
  • Drag your finger (or mouse) across squares to mark lots of squares with ✕ at once.
  • On a computer: right-click places or removes a piece. Arrow keys move, Space cycles a square, B places a piece, X toggles an ✕, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes, H gives a hint.
  • Every garden has exactly one answer.
