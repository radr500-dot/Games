How to play

  • Each puzzle hides a little picture. The numbers beside each row and above each column tell you how many squares in that line are filled.
  • Numbers like 3 · 1 mean a block of 3 filled squares, then a block of 1, in that order, with at least one empty square between them.
  • Choose Fill and tap a square to fill it. Choose Mark empty to put an ✕ on squares you know stay empty. Tap again to undo a square. You can drag along a row or column to do several at once.
  • When a line's numbers are all done, they fade so you can see what's left.
  • Stuck? Hint first points you to a good row or column, then shows which squares to fill and explains why.
  • There's no timer and nothing to lose. A wrong square is just a square you can tap again.
  • On a computer: right-click marks a square with ✕. Arrow keys move, Space fills, X marks, Backspace clears, Tab switches Fill and Mark, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes, H gives a hint.
  • Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone, no guessing.
