How to play

  • Steer by dragging left and right (or the arrow keys). Your band runs and throws on its own.
  • Blue gates add travelers or multiply them. Gray gates take some away, but every hit you land on a gray gate raises its number.
  • Orange gates make your band throw faster or farther for the rest of the stage.
  • Walls show how many hits they can take. If your band runs into one, the travelers at the front push it down, and you lose them.
  • Rival bands walk toward you. Knock them over before they reach you.
  • Coins drop from walls and crates. Spend them on upgrades between runs.
  • Past the finish, the bonus stairs multiply the coins you picked up.
  • There's no timer and nothing to lose. If your band runs out, you keep your coins and can try again right away.
